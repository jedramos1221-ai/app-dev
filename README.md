# The Amazing Spider-Man

**Genre:** Action, Superhero, Adventure  
**Main Characters:** Peter Parker (Spider-Man), Gwen Stacy, Dr. Curt Connors  

## Why I Love It
- Shows the balance between being a hero and a normal person  
- The emotional connection between Peter and Gwen feels real  
- The story explores responsibility and sacrifice deeply  

> “You’re going to make enemies. People will get hurt. Sometimes the people closest to you.” — Aunt May  


### Fun Facts
- Andrew Garfield plays a more emotional and realistic Spider-Man.  
- The movie was released in **2012** and directed by **Marc Webb**.  
- Its sequel, *The Amazing Spider-Man 2*, expanded on Peter’s backstory and relationships.  


### Favorite Scene
When Spider-Man swings through New York City at night — the visuals and music perfectly capture the freedom and power of being Spider-Man.
